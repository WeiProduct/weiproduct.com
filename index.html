Investment thesis

Consumer AI will be won inside repeated daily habits.

Generic assistants are powerful, but most people adopt software through specific moments: recording a thought, planning a day, learning a word, tracking health, or making a small financial decision. WeiProduct is built to discover and compound those moments quickly.
